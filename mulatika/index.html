The whole sub-commentary, one part per vibhaṅga. Its numbered paragraphs follow the Vibhaṅga paragraph that the Sammohavinodanī explains, and link to that paragraph in the commentary, the Pāli text and the Anuṭīkā. The words explained are in bold.
