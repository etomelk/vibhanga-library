Vibhaṅga-anuṭīkā

Vibhaṅga-anuṭīkā, ascribed to Ānanda’s pupil Dhammapāla · the text of the Sixth Buddhist Council (Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana), Yangon · digital edition of the Vipassana Research Institute

The whole Anuṭīkā, one part per vibhaṅga. Its numbered paragraphs follow the Vibhaṅga paragraph that the commentaries explain, and link to that paragraph in the Mūlaṭīkā, the Sammohavinodanī and the Pāli text. The words explained are in bold.
