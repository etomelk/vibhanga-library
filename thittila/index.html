The Book of Analysis (Vibhaṅga)

Translated from the Pāḷi of the Burmese Chaṭṭhasaṅgīti edition by Paṭhamakyaw Ashin Thiṭṭila (Seṭṭhila), Aggamahāpaṇḍita · Pali Text Society

The translation, one part per vibhaṅga as in the Pāli reader (so far the Analysis of the Aggregates to the Analysis of Dependent Origination, §1–354; the rest is being added). Each § is the Sixth Council paragraph and links to the same paragraph in the Pāli and the commentaries. The margin gives the pages of the printed translation and, marked P, of the PTS Pāli edition (Rhys Davids 1904), which Thiṭṭila prints in square brackets. The small lines under a paragraph are his notes (P¹ = see para. …).
