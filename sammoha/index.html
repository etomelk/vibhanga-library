Sammohavinodanī

Vibhaṅga-aṭṭhakathā (Sammohavinodanī nāma), ascribed to Buddhaghosa (5th century) · the text of the Sixth Buddhist Council (Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana), Yangon · digital edition of the Vipassana Research Institute

The whole commentary, one part per vibhaṅga (the longest cut at their sections). Each numbered paragraph explains the Vibhaṅga paragraph with the same number, and links to it in the Pāli text and to the sub-commentaries on it; those link back. The words explained are in bold, as in the edition. The margin gives the pages of the Myanmar edition and, marked P, of the PTS edition (A. P. Buddhadatta 1923), which Ñāṇamoli’s translation, The Dispeller of Delusion, gives in its margin.
