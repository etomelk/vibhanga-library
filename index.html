About this library

Reading

  • One part of a book per page, with its contents beside it.
  • Each book keeps its own numbering. Enter a number in the box at the top to go straight to it.
  • Every numbered section and printed page can be linked to.
  • Search finds words within a book; accents are optional, so rupa finds rūpa.
  • A book’s own index or glossary, where it has one, opens beside the text.

Between the books

  • Every book here shares the Sixth Council paragraph numbers of the Vibhaṅga (§1–1044): the commentaries are numbered by the paragraph they explain.
  • The small chips beside a § open the same paragraph in the other books.
  • Side by side shows any two books together, kept at the same paragraph.
  • The pages of the Myanmar edition, and (marked P) of the PTS editions, are in the margin.

The texts

  • The Pāli texts are those of the Sixth Council edition as the Vipassana Research Institute publishes them, unchanged.
  • The words a commentary explains are in bold, as in the edition; its variant readings are notes.
  • Your reading position and settings are stored only in this browser.
